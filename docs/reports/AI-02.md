# AI-02 — Deterministic goon state machine

**Status:** Accepted, Stage 1. **Owner:** Main Agent. **Date:** 23 October 2026.

Added a `CharacterBody3D` goon fixture with a capsule placeholder, perception child, and deterministic `TacticalAgent`. The agent emits movement destinations for patrol, investigation/search, pursuit, reposition, and flee; AI-03 navigation will consume those intents. Combat uses bounded configurable damage/cadence, reads current faction standing, and stops when targets become allied or unavailable. Health is held in `HealthState`; stun pauses attack cooldown; death is terminal. Transient authored hostility overrides do not mutate faction authority.

Defaults recorded in the task are engineering values, not approved balance: health 80, attack damage 10 at 1.25 s cadence, attack range 1.75 m, reposition below 0.9 m, flee threshold 25% health, movement intent speed 2.5 m/s, and search 3 s. The decision tick rejects steps above 0.25 s.
