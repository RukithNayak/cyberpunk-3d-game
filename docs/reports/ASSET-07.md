Accepted by Main Agent, 24 October 2026. The Blender source is editable, the optimized GLB imports in Godot 4.7.2, and the close-range fullscreen fixture shows the asset at human scale. The task-specific check passes. WORLD-06's integrated performance/package limits remain open.

## Delivered

- Blender source: [`night_market_hero.blend`](../../art/source/environment/night_market/night_market_hero.blend), generated by [`build_night_market_hero.py`](../../tools/blender/build_night_market_hero.py). The source contains 100 render mesh objects, 3 text objects, 3 collision objects and a preview rig. Blender evaluated the render set at 9,296 triangles and collision at 36 triangles. Runtime joining reduces the GLB to 13 mesh nodes total. Blender materials: 9; GLB materials: 10; GLB images: 6; GLB size: 18,325,680 bytes; source `.blend`: 17,703,957 bytes at the final source inspection.

## Accepted QA warnings and limits

- The 36 render warnings consist of flat cylinder-cap n-gons on small non-deforming hardware, intentional hidden face pairs where bolts/trim are seated into their mounts, and two concrete repair patches enclosed within the facade. The separate collision warning pairs are the adjoining left/right wall and lintel pieces. No QA errors remain.
- The shared 1K Metallic and AO helper maps are not connected to the exported GLB. Surface metallic is authored with material factors; contact shading comes from geometry and runtime lighting. These maps are not counted among the six embedded images.
- The 18.3 MB GLB is a single hero asset and has not been profiled in the full neighborhood. ASSET-04 budgets are comparison candidates only; WORLD-06 must measure integrated draw calls, frame timing and memory before ratifying limits.
- This delivers one hero storefront slice, not the neighborhood. Modular architecture, additional interiors, characters, merchant stock, hacking routes, and district assembly remain in ASSET-08/09, INV-07, HACK-07 and WORLD-08/09.
